# Questions For An Experienced Payroll Person

Last updated: 2026-06-17

Use these questions to verify the rules behind ESS pay-stub lines. The goal is to learn what the worker must record in the app so the app can predict gross additive pay.

## Main Question

On the ESS pay stub, some premium lines show a dollar amount while the summary hours cell is blank, and some detail pages may show `0:00` hours. For each sanitation premium pay type, can you tell me whether it is calculated by hours, by shift, by occurrence, by assignment, by day worked, by dump, by truck/equipment, or by route extension?

## Questions To Ask For Each Pay Type

1. What triggers this pay type?
2. Is it paid by actual hours, scheduled hours, one shift, one occurrence, one dump, one truck assignment, one route extension, or another unit?
3. Should ESS show hours on the summary page, the detail page, both, or neither?
4. Which date becomes the `Date Earned`, especially for a shift that crosses midnight?
5. What should a sanitation worker write down in the app to prove this pay type should appear?
6. Can this pay type stack with Saturday, Sunday, night differential, dump, route extension, loader, or holiday/chart/vacation work?

## Specific Pay Types To Verify

- Premium for Hours Worked Sunday (San-Worker)
- Premium for Hours Worked on Saturday (San-Worker)
- Hourly Night Shift Differential
- Hourly Saturday Night Shift Differential (San-Worker)
- Night Differential for Dump on Shift 1st Dump
- Saturday Night Differential for Dump on Shift 1st Dump
- 25 Cubic Yard Loader Hourly Differential
- 25 Cubic Yard Loader Hourly Differential on Saturday
- Night Differential for 25 Cubic Yard Loader
- Saturday Night Differential for 25 Cubic Yard Loader
- Premium for Hrs Wrkd on Sat-Route Ext.
- Hourly Sat Night Shift Diff-Route Ext.
- Hourly Night Shift Diff-Route Ext.
- Hourly Truck Differential - Route Extension

## Specific Rule Questions

1. What are the exact night-differential start and end times?
2. If a shift is `2400-0800`, which date controls Saturday/Sunday premium and night differential?
3. Does Saturday premium apply automatically to any Saturday work, or only specific tours/functions?
4. Does Sunday premium apply automatically to any Sunday work, or only specific tours/functions?
5. For dump money, does `I Dumped`, `Partner Dumped`, and `We Dumped` all pay the worker the same, or does who dumped matter?
6. If there are two dumps, does ESS show a separate second-dump line, a different amount, or a different pay description?
7. What exactly triggers loader pay?
8. What exactly triggers route extension, and when should route extension be turned off?
9. When a worker starts in one district and is sent to another district, which district/time/function should drive pay?
10. How do sick, APP/emergency, LODI, XWP, unavailable, detached, chart, and vacation statuses change paid hours or additive pay?
